Grandma's Secret Snow Cone Recipe‌​⁠​​​‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌​​‍‌‌‌​​‌​﻿​​​‍​​‌‌‌‌﻿​⁠​‌‌‍‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌‌‍​​‌‌‌‌​​‌​‍‌‌‌​‌‌​​​‌‍​
Ingredients: 2 cups shaved ice, 1/2 cup sugar, 1 cup water, a few drops of food coloring.​﻿‌‌‌‌​​​‌‍‌‌‌​​​‌‌‌​‍​​‌﻿‌‌‌​​​​﻿‍‌⁠‌‌​‌‌‌​‌⁠‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌​‍​​‌‌‌﻿‌​
Step 1: Dissolve the sugar in warm water, then stir in the food coloring.‌‌‌⁠​‍‌‌‌​‌‌⁠‌​​﻿‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌‌﻿​‌‍‌‌‌​‌​​‌​‌‍﻿​​﻿‌‌‌‌​‌‌​​‍‌‌‌​‌‌​​⁠​​‌‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌​
Step 2: Chill the syrup in the fridge for 30 minutes.‌‌‍‌‌‌​​‌​‌‌‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌​‌​‍‌‌‌​‌‌⁠​‌​​‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌​​‌‍‌‌‌​‌⁠​‌‌​‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌​‌​​⁠​‍‌‌‌
Step 3: Pack the shaved ice into a paper cup.​‌‌‌‌‌‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌​​‌‌‌‍‌‌‌​‌​‌​‌‌‌‍​​‌‌‌‌﻿​​‌‌​‍‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌​‍​​‌‌‌‌‌⁠‍‍‌‌‌​‌​​‌‍‌‍‌
Step 4: Pour the syrup slowly over the ice.‌‌‌​​‌​‌‍﻿​‌‌​‍​‌​﻿​‌‌​‌‍‌‍‌‌‍‌​‌‌‍‌‌‌​​‍​‌‌‍‌‌‌​‌​⁠​‍​‌‌‌‌​‌‌​‍‌‌⁠‌​​​‍​‌‌‍​‌‌‌
Step 5: Serve immediately with a spoon-straw.‌‌‌​​‌⁠‌‌‌​‍​‌​​‌​‌‌​‍​​​‌‍​​⁠‌‌​​‍‌‌‌‌‌​‍​‌‌‌‍‌‍‌​‌​​‌⁠‍‍‌‌​﻿​⁠‌‌​‌‍‌‍‍﻿‍‍‍
